Evidence exports

A regulator-ready pack for any complaint. Each export is itself recorded in the case's audit trail.

What's in a pack

  • Evidence PDF: cited payment trail, diagnosis, rule arithmetic, referral check, approval record
  • Source documents from the customer and the bank-record extracts pulled by the connector
  • Case file, full audit trail (tool inputs and outputs) and the decision log as JSON
  • MANIFEST.json with a SHA-256 hash of every file, so nothing can change unnoticed after export

Hashes prove integrity, not identity: packs are not digitally signed in this prototype.
